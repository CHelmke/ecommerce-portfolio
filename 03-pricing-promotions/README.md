# Pricing & Promotion Upload QA

## Objective
Prepare and validate a bulk Amazon price-discount upload before submission.

## Scenario
A fictional brand is planning a limited promotion across several SKUs. The workbook checks proposed prices against allowed minimum/maximum ranges, validates committed inventory, and calculates discount depth.

Amazon Promotions • Deals & Discounts • Bulk Upload QA • Pricing Controls • Data Validation • Spreadsheet Review • Operational Accuracy
