# Multichannel Launch, Contractor & Ecommerce Coordination

All names, products, quantities, budgets and dates are fictional. The included workbook is an original demonstration of common ecommerce workflows, not a modified employer template.

## Objective
Show how I keep ecommerce work moving when delivery depends on freelancers, specialists, agencies and other teams across Amazon, Walmart and Shopify.

## Launch workflow
I use a source-of-truth tracker with a clear owner, due date, work status, dependency/blocker, QA status, release gate and next action. "Complete" does not automatically mean "ready" - required deliverables must pass QA and dependencies must be resolved before launch.

A typical multichannel product launch may include:
- Amazon listing copy, required attributes, images and variation setup
- Walmart item setup, taxonomy and channel-specific attributes
- Shopify PDP build, mobile QA, inventory and checkout checks
- Creative work assigned to freelancers or designers
- Content work assigned to a VA or specialist
- Final verification after publishing rather than closing the task at submission

- **Launch Queue:** owners, due dates, blockers and release gates

## Example decisions
- Resolve an image-specification blocker and failed variation QA before release.
- Escalate a SKU-level inventory shortage rather than allowing surplus on another SKU to hide it.
- If advertising projects above the approved budget, calculate the remaining daily allocation and review inventory and margin before changing spend.
