# FBA Shipment Tracking & Reconciliation

## Objective
Track Amazon inbound shipments and quickly identify receiving discrepancies that need investigation.

## Scenario
A fictional brand has multiple FBA shipments in closed, receiving, checked-in and in-transit states. The goal is to compare expected units with located/received units and prioritize exceptions.

## Skills Demonstrated
FBA Shipment Planning • Shipment Monitoring • Reconciliation • Exception Investigation • Seller Central Operations • Follow-Up Prioritization • Excel
