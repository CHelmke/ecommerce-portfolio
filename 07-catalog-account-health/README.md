# Catalog & Account Health Investigation

## Objective
Demonstrate a structured response to an Amazon listing-compliance problem without exposing confidential account information.

## Fictional Scenario
Several products in a fictional catalog are suppressed after a marketplace review flags non-compliant product imagery. A major promotional event is approaching, creating both revenue and account-health risk.

## Investigation Workflow

1. **Confirm the issue**
   - Review the affected listings and account-health notification
   - Identify the exact policy or content element triggering the suppression
   - Separate confirmed marketplace feedback from assumptions

2. **Identify related exposure**
   - Search the broader catalog for listings using the same image/content pattern
   - Prioritize high-traffic and promotion-critical products
   - Record affected and at-risk SKUs in a remediation tracker

3. **Correct and document**
   - Coordinate compliant image/content updates
   - Open or update Seller Support cases where needed
   - Track owner, status, submission date and evidence

4. **Verify recovery**
   - Confirm listings return to active status
   - Monitor account health for additional violations
   - Validate that promotions/ads can resume where applicable

5. **Prevent recurrence**
   - Add the issue to catalog QA checklists
   - Update SOPs and training
   - Review related products proactively before major launches or events
