# Performance Interpretation Framework

A dashboard is useful only if it changes the next action. I separate marketplace performance into three questions.

## 1. Is this primarily a traffic problem?
Signals: low impressions, low sessions, weak click-through rate, or poor search visibility.

Possible actions: review search-term relevance, keyword coverage, advertising, organic rank, main-image/search presentation, and availability.

## 2. Is this primarily a conversion problem?
Signals: traffic is present but unit-session percentage / conversion rate is weak.

Possible actions: review price, offer, delivery promise, images, content, reviews, variations, structured attributes, and shopper objections.

## 3. Is demand healthy but operations creating risk?
Signals: low days of cover, stockout risk, budget pacing that exceeds available inventory, or promotional demand that is not supported by supply.

Possible actions: protect inventory, coordinate inbound timing, adjust promotional or advertising pressure, and escalate blockers before they become availability problems.

## Forecasting view
When forecasting against a plan, I compare projected revenue with the target while keeping advertising efficiency and inventory in the same view. A strong revenue forecast is not automatically a good outcome if it is driven by unprofitable spend or will create a stockout.
