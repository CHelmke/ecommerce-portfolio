# Listing optimization: fictional party bundle

**Recreated demonstration, not a client listing or measured performance result.** The brand, product and specifications below are fictional. Confirm product facts and current marketplace requirements before publishing.

## Goal
Help shoppers quickly understand the theme, guest count, contents and sizes. Reduce uncertainty without exaggerated claims or repetitive keywords.

## Before: deliberately weak sample
Space Party Birthday Decorations Supplies Set Plates Cups Party Kit Best Quality for Birthday Parties

Problems: repetitive wording, no guest count, no useful contents summary and an unsupported quality claim.

## Proposed title
Celebration Studio Space Birthday Party Supplies for 16 Guests - 67-Piece Set with Plates, Napkins, Cups and Tablecloths

## Proposed bullets

**Set the table for 16:** Includes 16 dinner plates, 16 dessert plates, 16 napkins and 16 cups, plus two tablecloths and one birthday sticker.

**Know exactly what is included:** The 67-piece count includes every individual item. Balloons, cutlery and banners are not included.

**Choose the sizes you need:** Dinner plates measure 9 inches; dessert plates measure 7 inches. Cups hold 9 oz, and each tablecloth measures 54 x 108 inches.

**Coordinate the party table:** A matching space theme brings the tableware together for a birthday celebration. Check the product images for the design on each item.

**Plan for a larger guest list:** One set provides a dinner plate, dessert plate, napkin and cup for each of 16 guests. Add another set when you need more place settings.

## Image and A+ brief
1. Main image: show the actual included items clearly on the required background; verify current category image rules. Do not imply excluded items are included.
2. Contents image: label each component and quantity; distinguish 16 place settings from 67 total pieces.
3. Dimensions image: show plate diameters, cup capacity and tablecloth dimensions.
4. Lifestyle image: show a realistic table setting; make decorative props and exclusions clear.
5. A+ content: lead with contents, then sizes and planning guidance. Use only approved brand assets; no unsupported safety, sustainability or licensing claims.

## Publish and measurement plan
- Confirm quantities, materials, dimensions, images, rights and product identifiers with approved source documents.
- Assign copy and imagery to named owners; review every deliverable against the brief before upload.
- Check variation relationships, category attributes, mobile readability, price, stock, delivery promise and live content after publication.
- Record the baseline, change date and any price, promotion, advertising or stock changes. Monitor sessions, click-through rate where available, unit session percentage, returns and customer questions.
- Use a controlled content experiment when eligible; otherwise compare comparable periods cautiously. A before/after change alone does not prove causation.

## Intended result
A clearer, more complete listing and a repeatable launch process. No sales lift or conversion improvement is claimed for this demonstration.
