# Example Multichannel Launch Scenario

A fictional party-supply SKU is scheduled to launch on Amazon, Walmart and Shopify.

| Channel | Deliverable | Owner | Status | Dependency / blocker | QA | Release gate | Next action |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Amazon | Parent/child variation | Marketplace Ops | Complete | None | Passed | Ready | Verify live relationship |
| Amazon | Main + secondary images | Freelance Designer | In progress | Waiting on final dimensions | Pending | Hold | Send confirmed dimensions and review against brief |
| Amazon | Title, bullets, attributes | Content VA | Complete | None | Needs revision | Hold | Correct guest-count wording and structured fields |
| Walmart | Item setup + taxonomy | Marketplace Ops | In progress | UPC confirmed | Pending | Hold | Complete required attributes and validate category |
| Shopify | PDP build | Web Contractor | In progress | Waiting on approved images | Pending | Hold | Finish PDP after image approval |
| Shopify | Launch QA | Marketplace Ops | Not started | PDP must be complete | Pending | Hold | Verify mobile, price, inventory and checkout |

## How I manage the work
I do not treat delegation as hand-off. I clarify requirements, document dependencies, follow up before due dates, review the finished work, return specific corrections when needed, and keep responsibility for the final live result.
