# Portfolio Data & Confidentiality Standard

This public portfolio contains **synthetic demonstration data, fictional brands, sanitized independent-project material, or clearly labeled public-listing observations**.

The projects recreate ecommerce workflows I have performed professionally or developed independently, but confidential client and employer data is not included.

## What is intentionally excluded

- Real confidential client or employer exports
- Customer or end-user information
- Private Seller Central / Vendor Central account identifiers
- Confidential shipment, case, financial or advertising data
- Proprietary pricing, sales, inventory or profit reports
- Passwords, credentials, API keys, access tokens or authentication details
- NDA-protected business information
- Former-employer templates or client-branded deliverables

Historical work may be used privately to remember the **workflow and analytical approach**, but it is recreated with fictional inputs before appearing here.

## Public marketplace observations

The Competitive ASIN Audit uses customer-facing Amazon listing observations. These values are labeled as a point-in-time snapshot because prices, review counts, Best Sellers Rank, availability, delivery promises and offer status can change.

## Independent project protection

The DigitalOpsDesk FBA Restock Planner is an independently developed product. The public portfolio includes a case study, documentation and visual previews, but not the full working source workbook.

## AI and productivity-tool use

Modern productivity tools, including AI assistance, may be used for drafting, formatting or development support. I review and validate the calculations, business logic, recommendations and final presentation before use.
