# Marketplace Performance & Forecasting

## Objective
Turn marketplace reporting into a prioritized action plan by reading revenue, advertising, traffic, conversion and inventory together.

## Scenario
This synthetic case study models a multi-product ecommerce account across a six-month reporting window. The workbook compares plan vs. projected revenue, product-level marketplace signals and channel budget pacing.

## Files
- `marketplace_performance_forecast_case_study.xlsx` - interactive workbook with Dashboard, Monthly Forecast, ASIN Performance, Budget Pacing and Assumptions tabs
- `assets/marketplace_performance_v2_preview.png` - dashboard preview
- `performance_interpretation.md` - framework for separating traffic, conversion and operational problems

## What the workbook demonstrates
- Plan vs. projected revenue forecasting
- Product-level performance diagnosis
- Sessions, CTR and conversion-rate interpretation
- ACOS and TACOS calculations
- Inventory days-cover risk
- Budget pacing and remaining daily allocation
- Action prioritization rather than reporting for its own sake

## Decision logic
A strong sales forecast is not automatically a good result. I keep advertising efficiency and inventory in the same view so I can distinguish between growth that is sustainable, growth that risks a stockout, and growth that is being bought inefficiently.
