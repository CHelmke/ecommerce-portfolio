# DigitalOpsDesk - Amazon FBA Restock Planner

## Independent project
I designed this Amazon FBA replenishment tool to turn Seller Central inventory data into clear shipment and reorder decisions.

The full working workbook is not included in this public portfolio because it is an independent product. The included Quick-Start Guide and screenshots show the workflow and logic without giving away the full source file.

## Business problem
Amazon sellers need to decide what to reorder, how much to ship, when to place the order, and whether an upcoming event will change demand. Raw marketplace reports do not automatically turn those inputs into an operational plan.

## What the planner does
- Imports an Amazon FBA inventory/replenishment report
- Stores product setup such as case packs, supplier information, cost, price, and lead-time overrides
- Supports multiple sales-velocity methods, including a weighted blend of recent periods
- Adds growth assumptions and promotional uplift for events such as Prime Day and BFCM
- Calculates days of cover, forecast demand, reorder timing, expected arrival, and recommended shipment quantity
- Rounds recommendations to case packs
- Can respect Amazon max-inventory or inbound-capacity limits
- Categorizes SKUs as Urgent, Watch, Healthy, or Hold
- Produces a dashboard and shipment-ready output
- Includes optional Google Apps Script functions for sorting, alerts, CSV export, refresh, and backup
